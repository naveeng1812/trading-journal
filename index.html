Questions, answered

Do I need to connect my broker?
No. You log trades manually — symbol, direction, entry, exit, and the journal handles the rest. Broker integration is on the roadmap but not required to get started.
Which markets are supported?
Forex pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY), indices (US30, SPX500, NAS100), commodities (XAUUSD gold, WTI crude), crypto (BTCUSD, ETHUSD), and US stocks (AAPL, TSLA, and more). If your instrument isn't listed, you can type it in manually.
Is my trading data private?
Yes. Each account is isolated — your trades are tied to your login and nobody else can see them. We don't share or sell your data.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. The journal is fully responsive and works in any mobile browser. Log trades from your phone right after you close a position.
Is there really a free plan?
Yes — the Free plan is free forever, with no trial period and no credit card required. You get unlimited trade logging, the calendar heatmap, and basic analytics. Upgrade to Pro or Elite only if you need more.
